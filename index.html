Questions

Why does my site show blank?

Some sites tell browsers not to display them inside other pages. If yours does, allow embedding from your own host, or upload a screenshot instead.

Do you store my URLs or images?

No. Everything runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded or saved.

Will localhost work?

Yes, if it is reachable from the device you are using right now.
